🧔 Policy for Appointment and Responsibilities of Technical Assistant (TA)

  1. Purpose of the Policy
    • The purpose of this policy is to define the eligibility criteria, roles, responsibilities, ethical standards, and evaluation mechanism for students appointed as Technical Assistants (TAs) in undergraduate and postgraduate courses.
    • The TA role is intended to enhance the academic learning experience of students while maintaining fairness, transparency, and academic integrity.
  2. Eligibility Criteria
    • The applicant must be currently enrolled as a student at FAST NUCES.
    • The applicant must have successfully completed the course for which they are applying as a TA.
    • The applicant must have achieved at least an A– or A grade in the relevant subject.
    • The applicant must have a strong academic standing with no active disciplinary action.
    • The applicant must demonstrate:
      • Sound conceptual understanding of the subject
      • Ability to communicate technical concepts clearly
      • Professional conduct and ethical responsibility
    • Final selection is solely at the discretion of the Course Instructor.
  3. Appointment Nature and Duration
    • The TA position is temporary and course-specific.
    • Appointment is valid only for the duration of the semester unless terminated earlier.
    • The role does not constitute employment by FAST NUCES.
    • The Course Instructor reserves the right to terminate the TA appointment at any time without prior notice if policy violations occur.
  4. Roles and Responsibilities of the Technical Assistant
    • Academic Support
      • Assist the Course Instructor in clarifying course concepts to students.
      • Support labs, tutorials, or discussion sessions (if assigned).
      • Respond to student academic queries professionally and within reasonable time.
    • Assignment & Assessment Development
      • Design assignments, quizzes, and problem scenarios under the guidance of the Course Instructor.
      • Prepare complete and correct solutions for all assessments.
      • Ensure:
        • Alignment with course learning outcomes
        • Appropriate difficulty level
        • No plagiarism or reuse of unauthorized material
      • All assignments and solutions must be approved by the Course Instructor before release.
  5. Grading and Assessment Policy
    • All grading must be conducted using a clearly defined assessment rubric approved by the Course Instructor.
    • Grading must be:
      • Fair
      • Consistent
      • Transparent
      • Free from bias or favoritism
    • Grading timelines:
      • Assignments and quizzes must be graded within 5 working days of submission deadline.
    • Marks must be properly documented and shared with the Course Instructor.
    • Any grading disputes must be immediately reported to the Course Instructor.
  6. Ethical Conduct and Confidentiality
    • Maintain confidentiality of:
      • Student grades
      • Solutions
      • Exam or quiz content
      • Any internal course materials
    • No sharing of solutions, rubrics, or assessment materials with students before official release.
    • Avoid conflict of interest, including:
      • Grading close friends
      • Providing unfair academic advantage
    • Any form of academic dishonesty, favoritism, plagiarism, or data manipulation will result in immediate termination.
  7. Performance Evaluation
    • The TA’s performance will be evaluated based on:
      • Timely completion of assigned tasks
      • Quality of assignments and solutions
      • Accuracy and fairness of grading
      • Professional behavior and communication
    • Feedback from students may be considered, but final judgment rests with the Course Instructor.
  8. Compensation Policy
    • The TA position does not guarantee financial compensation.
    • No payment will be provided in case of:
      • Unfair or biased assessment
      • Delayed grading
      • Poor-quality solutions
      • Violation of ethical standards
      • Failure to meet responsibilities
    • Any compensation (if applicable) is conditional upon satisfactory performance as determined by the Course Instructor.
  9. Disciplinary Actions and Termination
    • The Course Instructor reserves the right to:
      • Issue warnings
      • Reassign responsibilities
      • Terminate the TA role immediately
    • Grounds for termination include (but are not limited to):
      • Repeated delays
      • Unfair grading
      • Breach of confidentiality
      • Misconduct or unprofessional behavior
  10. Acknowledgment and Acceptance
    • By accepting the role of Technical Assistant, the student acknowledges that they have read and understood this policy.
    • They agree to comply with all rules and responsibilities.
    • Failure to comply may result in termination and possible academic consequences.
